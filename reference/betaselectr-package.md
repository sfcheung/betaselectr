# betaselectr: Betas-Select in Structural Equation Models and Linear Models

It computes betas-select, coefficients after standardization in
structural equation models and regression models, standardizing only
selected variables. Supports models with moderation, with product terms
formed after standardization. It also offers confidence intervals that
account for standardization, including bootstrap confidence intervals as
proposed by Cheung et al. (2022)
[doi:10.1037/hea0001188](https://doi.org/10.1037/hea0001188) . An
introduction to the package can be found in Sun et al. (2026)
[doi:10.1080/00273171.2026.2672692](https://doi.org/10.1080/00273171.2026.2672692)
.
